# Slide 2 — What Problem Are We Solving?

**Kicker:** The Multi-Trillion Dollar Crisis in Human Competence

**Headline:** What Problem Are We Solving? AI Is Causing Human Cognitive Atrophy & Learning Hasn't Evolved

## The Memory Paradox
Reliance on external digital tools and AI can weaken our internal memory systems — even as those same tools enhance our cognitive capabilities.
*Source: Oakley et al., 2025*

## Cognitive Offloading
Skipping retrieval, integration, and pattern recognition by consulting AI immediately weakens declarative and procedural memory — the intellectual passenger effect.

## Atrophied Mechanisms
Bypassing spaced repetition and retrieval practice starves the brain of the durable memory "schemata" and neural manifolds needed for critical thinking & skill mastery.

## Structural Failure
Industrial 1:many classrooms and EdTech 1.0 streaming (Coursera/Udemy) yield <6% completion rates, compounding the reversal of the Flynn Effect.

## Societal Decline
The paradox is linked to the reversal of the Flynn Effect — IQ scores in developed nations plateauing or declining as discovery-based learning combines with rising cognitive offloading.

## Education Hasn't Evolved in Years
Classrooms were built for economics, not achievement — a compromise for not being able to give everyone their own tutor. Despite AI's rise, learning is still one-to-many, and unguarded chatbots make it worse: cognitive offloading means learning less, from a source you can't always trust — just as demand for accurate, trustworthy learning has never been higher.

## The Core Risk
"The easier it is to look up an answer with AI, the weaker your organic memory becomes. Workers become helpless intellectual passengers, unable to audit hallucinations or reason without a prompt bar."
